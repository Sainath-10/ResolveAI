# Subscription Policy

## Plans and billing
- Basic: 7.99 USD per month.
- Plus: 14.99 USD per month.
- Pro: 149.00 USD per year.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled.

## Cancellation
Customers can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period; access continues until then. The account shows `cancel_at_period_end` when a cancellation is scheduled. Partial months are not refunded.

## Failed payments
A failed payment moves the subscription to `past_due`. Customers keep a **7-day grace period** to update their payment method. If payment still fails, the subscription is suspended and paid features stop. Updating a card is a customer action; support can explain where to do it but cannot charge a new card on the customer's behalf.

## Renewals
Renewal reminders are sent 7 days before the renewal date. Annual plans renew once per year on the anniversary date.

## Refunds
Subscription fees are refundable only when a duplicate charge or a billing error is confirmed. Change-of-mind requests are not refunded for the current period. Disputes are reviewed by a human.

## Plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
