# Refund and Return Policy

## Refund window
Customers may request a refund within **30 calendar days of delivery**. The window starts on the delivery date recorded on the order, not the order date. Requests outside the window require human review.

## Eligibility
A refund is eligible when all of the following are true:
- The order status is `delivered`.
- Delivery happened no more than 30 days ago.
- The item is not digital and not marked final sale.
- No refund has already been processed for the order.

## Non-refundable items
Digital products, gift cards, and items marked final sale are not refundable. Opened hygiene products and personalised items are also excluded.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If an item arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped, the customer may choose a replacement or a full refund within 30 days of delivery. A support agent should verify the claim against the order and any existing claim before promising an outcome.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds return to the original payment method in **5-10 business days**. The time depends on the card issuer or bank, not on the store.

## What support may promise
Support may explain eligibility and next steps. Support must not state that a refund has been issued, processed, or approved until a refund record exists. Refund exceptions are escalated to a human reviewer.
