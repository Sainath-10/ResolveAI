# Delivery Policy

## Shipping options
- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days after dispatch.
- Express shipping: 1-2 business days after dispatch.
- Digital orders are delivered by email immediately and are not shipped.

## Tracking
A tracking number is added when an order is dispatched. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. A missing update does not by itself mean the parcel is lost.

## Delayed orders
An order is considered delayed when it is more than **2 business days past its estimated delivery date**. For delayed orders, support should confirm the carrier and tracking status before offering options.

## Lost parcels
If tracking shows no movement for **7 business days**, or the carrier marks the parcel lost, support may offer a replacement or refund. Lost-parcel refunds are recorded as an exception and require human confirmation.

## Address changes
Address changes are possible only before dispatch. After dispatch, the customer must contact the carrier. Support cannot reroute a parcel that is already in transit.

## Damaged on arrival
Damage discovered at delivery follows the damaged-item process in the refund policy: replacement or refund within 30 days, after the order and claim details are verified.
